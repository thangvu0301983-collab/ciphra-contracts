CIPHRA is building an FHE coprocessor network that lets AI models answer questions without ever reading them: prompts are encrypted on the user's device, computed on as ciphertext by independent nodes, and decrypted only by the user. CPH is its token. Fair launch on Programmable launch protection v1: 100% of the 1,000,000,000 supply is placed in the Uniswap v4 pool at launch and locked forever; no team, presale, investor or airdrop allocation; the launch wallet makes one small opening buy. Launch protection: opening valuation at least USD 15,000; for the first 300 seconds every wallet, including the developer, can buy or receive at most 2% of the supply; the total buy fee starts at 99% and its extra part falls linearly to zero over 30 seconds, paid to the Programmable treasury (the launch wallet is exempt from that extra part only); sells pay normal fees. Fees on every swap, in ETH, rounded up per trade: 0.30% Programmable platform fee plus a fixed 2% creator fee on buys and on sells, both immutable, so 2.3% all-in after the first 30 seconds. Creator fees go to the Cipher Vault, which anyone can trigger to collect and distribute: 50% Sealed Pot to the route set by the vault ballot contract (buyback and burn, compute grants, or node rewards), 30% R&D treasury, 20% Break-the-Cipher bounty pool paid out by the guardian. No ballot is set at launch: the launch graph contains only the Cipher Vault, its ballot address is empty, and the Sealed Pot route stays at buyback and burn until a ballot is bound. The planned Blind Ballot (a commit-reveal vote weighted by CipherStake stake held before each 7-day epoch opens) is published in the source repository but is not part of the launch graph and is not reviewed or verified by Programmable; it can only be deployed after launch because it needs the token address. Whatever address is bound as the ballot can set the Sealed Pot route among the three fixed recipients, so the first binding relies on the guardian; when a new route is written, fees earned under the old route are distributed first. At launch one ordinary wallet, 0x8B4b6d4AB1Ad29E22cA5Fb850E8959a7439D944F, not a multisig, is the guardian and receives every vault share (buyback, compute, nodes, R&D). Guardian powers: bind the first ballot once, immediately and without a timelock; replace the ballot, change recipients or change itself only after a 48-hour public timelock; pay bounties from the bounty pool; and pause the ballot (safe mode, route frozen at burn). No contract spends vault ETH at a market price: buybacks, burns and grants are executed manually by that wallet and announced. Nothing can mint or pause trading; after the 300-second window nothing limits transfers.
